# Prompts para ChatGPT: galería "Dynamic Reset 3×3"

## Cómo usarlo (en este orden)

1. **Abre un chat nuevo en ChatGPT** y adjunta 4 imágenes:
   - la ilustración cartoon del yoga (referencia de ESTILO),
   - la ilustración anatómica del lunge (referencia de MÚSCULOS),
   - la nueva foto de ella (referencia de CARA y PELO),
   - `00_hoja_de_contacto.png` (referencia de POSES).
   - (Para los ejercicios con aro 3.2 y 3.3 adjunta además `ref_aro_original.jpg`, tu imagen original.)
2. Pega el **PROMPT 1 (personaje)**. Repite hasta que te guste la hoja de personaje y guárdala.
3. En el **mismo chat**, pega el **PROMPT 2 (reglas fijas)** una sola vez.
4. Luego pide **un ejercicio por mensaje** con el PROMPT 3, adjuntando los 2 PNG de ese ejercicio desde esta carpeta (por ejemplo `09_…pos1` y `10_…pos2`). Así cada imagen respeta la pose exacta.
5. Si el personaje empieza a cambiar, vuelve a adjuntar la hoja de personaje y escribe: "Same character as the character sheet, keep face, hair and outfit identical."

> Los prompts están en inglés porque los generadores de imágenes responden mejor así. Los textos del PDF (INHALA/EXHALA, repeticiones) los agregamos después. Por eso se pide que la imagen salga **sin texto**.

## PROMPT 1: hoja de personaje

```
Create a CHARACTER SHEET for a fitness guide, using the 3 reference images I attached:

1) STYLE (cartoon yoga illustration): clean vector cartoon, warm dark-brown outlines of medium weight, flat colors with one soft cel-shade tone, gentle friendly proportions (head slightly larger than realistic, about 1/6.5 of body height), soft rounded shapes, white background.
2) ANATOMY (orange muscle illustration): when a muscle is "in focus" it is drawn with its real anatomical shape, filled in bright orange (#F26A1F) with fine darker fiber lines following the real fiber direction and a thin dark-orange outline, visible on top of the clothing, exactly like that reference. Secondary/helper muscles use the same treatment in warm yellow (#F6B23A). Non-focus muscles are NOT drawn.
3) FACE & HAIR (photo of her): use her facial features: face shape, eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips, smile, skin tone, hair color and texture, and any characteristic details (braids, earrings, freckles/moles). Make her body slightly fitter and slimmer than in the photo: athletic, toned, healthy, not exaggerated.

Outfit (always the same): charcoal-grey sports bra (#3C3F47) and high-waisted charcoal 7/8 leggings, barefoot. For training, her hair is tied in a ponytail (keep her braid details).

Show the same woman in 4 views on one sheet, standing neutral: front, right side profile (facing right), back, and 3/4 view. No muscles highlighted, no text, no logos, white background.
```

## PROMPT 2: reglas fijas (pégalo una vez, después del personaje)

```
From now on, every image you make is for the same fitness guide and must follow these rules:
- SAME character as the character sheet (identical face, hair/ponytail, skin tone, outfit, body type).
- SAME style: vector cartoon, warm brown outlines, flat colors + one cel-shade tone.
- Format: one wide image (3:2) split into TWO panels side by side with a thin light-grey divider: LEFT = position 1, RIGHT = position 2 of the same exercise. Same camera, same scale and same floor line in both panels so the movement is easy to compare.
- Background: white; floor = thin light-grey line, or a lavender yoga mat (#D7CBF3) when she is on the floor.
- Muscles: draw ONLY the listed focus muscles in orange (#F26A1F) and helper muscles in yellow (#F6B23A), anatomically shaped with fiber lines, over the clothes, same muscles highlighted in both panels.
- Movement arrows: simple navy-blue arrows (#2B4C9B) with a white outline, only where I ask.
- Props: dumbbells = small lilac hexagonal 3 kg dumbbells; resistance bands = pink (#F0609A); Pilates ring = purple ring with two pads.
- Anatomically and technically correct exercise form is the top priority: follow the joint angles of the pose reference images I attach exactly.
- NO text, letters, numbers or labels inside the image.
```
