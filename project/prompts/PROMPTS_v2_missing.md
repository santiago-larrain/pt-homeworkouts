## Tres trucos que resuelven el problema de izquierda y derecha

1. **No uses "izquierda/derecha de ella".** ChatGPT las confunde. Usa:
   - **izquierda/derecha DE LA IMAGEN** ("toward the LEFT edge of the picture");
   - **lado CERCANO / LEJANO** ("NEAR arm = the one closest to the viewer, drawn in front of her body and fully visible"; "FAR arm = behind her body, partly hidden").
2. **Haz la posición 2 EDITANDO la posición 1.** Primero genera solo la posición 1 (una sola imagen, no dos paneles). Luego, en el mismo chat: *"Edit this exact image. Keep EVERYTHING identical (character, camera, mat, the other arm and the other leg). Change ONLY…"*. Al cambiar una sola cosa, ChatGPT ya no se equivoca de extremidad. Al final yo junto los dos paneles en el PDF.
3. **Adjunta el diagrama.** Para el Dead Bug y la Rotación externa te dejé un diagrama de palitos con el lado cercano en **azul** y el lejano en **gris**. Adjúntalo y di: *"Follow the attached diagram exactly: blue limbs = near side, grey limbs = far side."*

## Reglas base: pégalas al inicio de un chat nuevo

```
We are continuing a fitness-guide illustration series. Attached: (1) the character sheet, (2) a finished illustration from the series as the STYLE ANCHOR.
Every new image MUST match the style anchor exactly:
- same woman: same face, dark-brown hair in a high ponytail with a small braid along the side of the head, warm tan skin, slim athletic build;
- same outfit: pink/mauve leaf-pattern sports bra and high-waisted pink/mauve leaf-pattern leggings, barefoot;
- same cartoon style: brown outlines, soft flat shading, white background, lavender yoga mat (#C9B8F0) when on the floor, light-grey floor line when standing;
- focus muscles drawn with their real anatomical shape and fibre lines, in ORANGE; helper muscles in YELLOW; drawn over the clothes;
- navy-blue arrows for movement only;
- NO text, no letters, no numbers, no labels in the picture.
Never mirror the pose and never swap limbs. When I say LEFT/RIGHT I always mean the left/right of the PICTURE, not of her body. NEAR = the limb closest to the viewer (in front of her body, fully visible). FAR = the limb on the other side (behind her body, partly hidden).
Reply only with the image.
```
